FAQ

Clear answers before you install.

Does the free trial require payment authorization?

No. The trial is email-backed through ExtensionPay. Users sign in with email, start the 7-day trial, and then can pay later if they choose.

Can existing users log in with the same email?

Yes. Existing paid users can continue by logging in with the same payment email tied to their ExtensionPay account.

Is everything paid?

No. WA Web Plus keeps creation free for templates, tabs, quick replies, and lists. Payment is enforced when users execute premium actions like large sends, scheduling, exports, and live automation.

Do you store WhatsApp messages on your servers?

No. WA Web Plus is designed as a local-first browser extension. Your WhatsApp data stays in your browser environment unless you explicitly export it.
