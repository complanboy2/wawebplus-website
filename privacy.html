Privacy

Privacy policy

This policy covers our browser extensions WA Web Utils and WA Utility Kit (together, “the extensions”) and this website. Both extensions share the same code and the same local-first privacy model, described below.

Products covered

  • WA Web Utils, available on the Chrome Web Store.
  • WA Utility Kit (WA Utility Kit - CRM, Bulk & Chat Tools), available on the Chrome Web Store.

Both extensions work with WhatsApp Web in Chrome. They are independent extensions and are not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta. Each extension keeps its own separate data in your browser.

Core principle

The extensions are built to work inside your browser. Customer conversation data stays local in your browser unless you export or download it, or you use one of the optional features listed below that send data to a service. We do not run servers that receive your conversation data, and we do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Data we may process

  • Extension settings, local workspace data, notes, templates, tags, reminders, and scheduled actions stored in the browser (browser extension storage and IndexedDB). Some small settings are kept in Chrome’s extension sync storage, which Chrome may sync between browsers where you are signed in to the same Chrome profile.
  • Deleted-message history captured after the feature is enabled, stored locally for Settings review and cleanup.
  • Billing and account identity handled through ExtensionPay for login, payment, and subscription management.
  • Text you choose to translate, only when you use inline translation (see below).
  • Contact or deal details, only when you set up the optional CRM sync (see below).
  • Message text, only when you set up an optional AI service (see below).
  • Support emails or troubleshooting details you send directly to support.

Billing and authentication

Login, payment, and subscription status are handled through ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which processes payments on our behalf. When you pay or log in, ExtensionPay receives the email address and payment details you enter on its pages; we do not receive your card details. The extensions store the resulting account state locally so features can be unlocked correctly. Offline payments (for example UPI or PayPal) are confirmed by support, who may issue an activation key bound to your chat account number.

Inline translation (MyMemory)

If you use the inline translation feature, the text of the message you choose to translate (up to 500 characters) and the target language are sent to the MyMemory translation service (api.mymemory.translated.net, operated by Translated) to get the translation. This happens only when you use translation; nothing is sent automatically. If you have set up your own AI service (see below), translation requests go to that service first. MyMemory’s own terms and privacy policy apply to text it receives.

Optional CRM sync

CRM sync is off unless you configure it. If you connect HubSpot with your own access token, the contact and deal details handled by the sync are sent to HubSpot (api.hubapi.com). If you instead configure a Google Sheets script URL or a webhook URL, the same sync data is sent only to the URL you entered. CSV export creates a file on your device and sends nothing.

Optional AI features

AI reply suggestions, chat summaries, tone adjustment, and AI translation need an AI service that you configure. When you set one up, the relevant message text (for example messages from the chat you summarise, or the draft you adjust) is sent only to the endpoint address you entered, together with the API key you provided. No AI data is sent anywhere if you have not configured an endpoint.

Voice note transcription

If you use voice note transcription, the extensions use your browser’s built-in speech recognition. In Google Chrome, speech recognition is provided by Google and audio may be processed by Google under Chrome’s terms.

Website analytics

The website may use Google Analytics to understand page visits and clicks on install, login, pricing, and support links. This helps measure which pages lead to Chrome Web Store installs and improve the website. Browser-level privacy controls such as Do Not Track may limit this measurement. The extensions themselves do not include analytics.

Deleted-message history

When enabled, the extensions may keep a local browser-side history of captured deleted messages so you can review them in Settings. This data stays on your device and can be cleared from the extension UI.

Exports, backups, and removal

Exports, backups, and reports are created on your device and saved by your browser; they are not uploaded by the extensions. Uninstalling an extension removes its extension storage. Workspace data kept in the chat website’s browser storage (IndexedDB) can be removed by clearing site data for web.whatsapp.com in your browser settings.

Support communication

If you contact support, we may use the details you share, such as your billing email, screenshots, or issue description, only to troubleshoot, respond, and resolve your request.
