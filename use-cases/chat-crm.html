FAQ

WA Web CRM questions.

What does WA Web Utils add to WA Web?

It adds practical CRM workflows around the WA Web workspace, including lead context, notes, tags, templates, follow-up reminders, scheduling, and exports.

Who should use this CRM workflow?

It is built for sales, support, admissions, services, and local-business teams that already manage customer conversations from WA Web in Chrome.
