FAQ

WhatsApp Web CRM questions.

What does WA Web CRM add to WhatsApp Web?

It adds practical CRM workflows around the browser chat workspace, including lead context, notes, tags, templates, follow-up reminders, scheduling, and exports.

Who should use this CRM workflow?

It is built for sales, support, admissions, services, and local-business teams that already manage customer conversations from WhatsApp Web in Chrome.
