Safety guide

Send campaigns without risking your number.

01

Message only opted-in contacts

People should have asked to hear from you. Unsolicited bulk messaging is the main reason numbers get reported and restricted.

02

Pace your sends

Spread messages over time with random delays instead of sending hundreds at once.

03

Avoid duplicates

Sending the same message twice to one person looks like spam. Use duplicate checks.

04

Personalize

Use the name and a relevant detail. Identical text sent to many people is easy to flag.

05

Make opting out easy

Honor stop requests immediately and keep a do-not-message list.

06

Start small

Warm up a number with smaller batches and watch for replies and blocks.
