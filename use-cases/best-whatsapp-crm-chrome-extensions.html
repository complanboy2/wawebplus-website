Buyer guide

How to choose a web chat CRM extension.

01

Where is your data stored?

Prefer tools that keep notes, tags and templates in your browser unless you choose to export or sync them.

02

What permissions does it ask for?

Read the permissions on the store listing. A CRM sidebar should not need broad access to every site.

03

What happens when the chat app changes?

Extensions depend on the web app. Check how quickly the developer ships fixes and read recent reviews.

04

Is pricing predictable?

Look for clear monthly, six-month and yearly plans and a free plan you can test before you commit.

05

Can you export your data?

You should be able to export contacts, templates and reports to CSV and restore a backup.

06

Is there real support?

Look for a support page, an email address and a changelog.
