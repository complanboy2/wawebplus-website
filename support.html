Help topics

Common support paths.

Each topic follows the same support model: identify the account, describe the action, and share enough context to reproduce the issue.

01

Billing and login

Use your original payment email through the in-extension login flow to restore access.

02

Manual activation

Use support activation only when WA Web Plus support explicitly gives you a manual key.

03

Contact support

Email support@wa-plus.app with your payment email and issue summary.

04

Message any number

If you enter a local number without a country code, the extension uses your logged-in linked chat number for context.

05

Deleted history

Deleted-message records appear in Settings when the feature is enabled and can be cleaned there.

06

Bulk sending

Keep the browser chat workspace open and connected while sending. WA Web Plus reports failures instead of pretending a send worked.

Support checklist

Include the email you used for payment or login, your extension version, and the exact screen or action that failed.

When to attach screenshots

Attach screenshots for UI problems, deleted-message history problems, and any case where the screen state matters.

Broadcast issue details

For bulk or broadcast issues, mention whether the browser chat workspace was open, connected, and ready at send time.

Guide

Good reports get fixed faster.

The most useful support request describes what you expected, what happened instead, where it happened, and which account or linked chat number was involved.
